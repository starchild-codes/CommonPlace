# Theme packs

Commonplace v0.3 groups journal pieces into themed drawers instead of asking someone to hunt through one enormous asset library.

A built-in pack is intentionally lightweight: it is a name, a palette, a suggested paper tone, and a set of search tags. The assets themselves remain ordinary reusable journal pieces, so the same moon can appear in a space pack, a purple pack, or a midnight pack without being duplicated.

## Built-in packs

The app ships original packs across six buckets:

- story-ish vibes
- aesthetics
- hobbies
- places
- sports
- colors

The story-ish packs are original atmosphere packs rather than official franchise packs. That keeps the repository distributable and avoids pretending that fan art, actor photos, logos, or studio artwork belong to this project.

## Custom packs

The custom pack maker is the escape hatch for specific obsessions. A user can:

- name a pack anything they like;
- choose an emoji and color;
- collect up to 40 built-in journal pieces;
- add up to eight small personal images;
- reopen the pack later from the Theme Packs tab.

Custom packs are stored in browser local storage. Personal images are limited to 700 KB each so one pack does not quietly fill the browser's storage quota.

## Why group assets at all?

A giant unstructured sticker wall quickly becomes tiring. Theme packs use recognition and meaningful grouping: “ocean,” “cricket,” or “dark academia” is a faster starting point than repeatedly searching individual objects. The pack is a shortcut, not a rule. Users can mix pieces from any pack immediately.
